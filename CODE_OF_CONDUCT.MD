# Code of Conduct

We want Hawkeye-Zero to be a welcoming and constructive community for everyone.
Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private
  information without consent.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Reports can be sent privately to a project maintainer through the
contact method listed in the repository. Include relevant links or details when
possible. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled in a timely and
fair manner. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project.
